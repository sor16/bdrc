# Jokulsa a Fjollum gauging station in Iceland

Data on discharge and stage from Jokulsa a Fjollum gauging station in
Iceland

## Usage

``` r
jokfjoll
```

## Format

A data frame with columns:

- `W`:

  Measurements of water stage in meters

- `Q`:

  Measurements of water discharge in cubic meters per second

## Source

Icelandic Meteorological Office, Landsvirkjun - the National Power
Company of Iceland, and the Icelandic Road and Coastal Administration.
